About Offroad Tactical · Michigan

Original designs.
Hands-on fabrication.

Offroad Tactical is a Michigan-based business focused on Humvee parts, practical upgrades, and custom metalwork. We bring design and fabrication together to turn ideas into parts with a clear purpose.

Our approach starts with the details: what a part needs to accomplish, how it mounts, how it fits the vehicle, and how it can be manufactured. From the first concept to the finished piece, we care about both function and appearance.

01 / Design

Designed from scratch.

Our original parts begin with an idea and take shape through CAD design. Working digitally allows us to develop geometry, plan mounting locations, and consider material thickness, bends, and assembly before cutting metal.

We design with the finished product in mind. Access to hardware, installation space, clean edges, and the relationship between adjoining components all help shape a thoughtful design.

02 / Fabrication

From CAD to metal.

Fabrication brings those designs into the physical world. Cutting, forming, and welding each play a role in turning flat material into a finished component.

Our hands-on approach connects what happens on the screen with what happens at the workbench. That connection helps us refine designs around the realities of fabrication and gives us a direct role in the details that define the final piece.

Purpose in every part

Built around the Humvee.

The Humvee’s distinctive construction creates opportunities for purposeful upgrades. Our focus includes parts that improve usability, add personal style, and complement the vehicle’s rugged character.

  1. 01 — PURPOSE

    Start with the need.

    A useful part starts with a clear goal, whether that means organizing the interior, adding a mounting point, or changing an exterior detail.

  2. 02 — DETAILS

    Consider the whole part.

    Material, shape, mounting access, and appearance all matter. Each decision contributes to how a component fits into the larger build.

  3. 03 — CRAFT

    Connect design and making.

    Understanding how a part will be cut, bent, and assembled helps turn a good concept into a practical fabrication project.
