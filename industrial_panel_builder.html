Industrial Panel Build Configurator

This single-file webpage lets a customer configure an enclosure and see a generated panel layout preview. It includes enclosure size, row count, PLC, power supply, surge suppression, I/O, safety relay, fuses, and terminals. The preview updates automatically and places components on the selected DIN rail rows.
